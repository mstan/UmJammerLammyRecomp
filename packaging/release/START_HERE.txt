Um Jammer Lammy Recompiled @VERSION@

Run Um_Jammer_Lammy_Recompiled.exe on Windows, or the AppImage on Linux.
Choose your own US SCUS-94448 disc CUE, keeping its original BIN beside it.
Bundled OpenBIOS is available automatically. No retail BIOS or disc is included.

Internal Resolution defaults ON at 4x. Mods offers saved Native (1x) through
32x choices; disabling this mod uses native resolution. Adaptive Widescreen
and title-owned Fast Resource Loading retain their existing defaults.
Generic CD Speed and Fast Loading packages are not shipped.

Rhythm Timing Assist remains optional and OFF by default. Enable it in Mods
only if wanted; the original timing and scoring remain available. The audio
buffer target is 60 ms. Adjust timing compensation to your own display/audio.
Preserve your native memory cards in saves and your existing personal settings.

The fresh profile-training boot is a build step, not new gameplay/audio or
per-title performance qualification. Complete stage/duet/PaRappa-mode coverage
is not claimed. See README and release notes for the retained preview scope.
